printing

python.Printing.natural

Section titled “python.Printing.natural”

The decimal digits of the natural n.

python.Printing.integer

Section titled “python.Printing.integer”

The decimal text of the int n.

python.Printing.join

Section titled “python.Printing.join”

The pieces joined with separator between them.

python.Printing.hexDigit

Section titled “python.Printing.hexDigit”

The digit d (0-15) in lowercase hexadecimal.

python.Printing.hexWithin

Section titled “python.Printing.hexWithin”

The natural n in lowercase hexadecimal, within fuel digits (the digits so far, after, after it).

python.Printing.hex

Section titled “python.Printing.hex”

The natural n in lowercase hexadecimal.

python.Printing.address

Section titled “python.Printing.address”

The address an object without a repr of its own shows: 0x and its heap position, spaced like CPython’s.

python.Printing.trimmedWithin

Section titled “python.Printing.trimmedWithin”

Significant digits without trailing zeros, the exponent of the last digit raised to match (within fuel).

python.Printing.shortestWithin

Section titled “python.Printing.shortestWithin”

The fewest significant digits (from count up to 17) that read back to the finite float v exactly, trying at most fuel more counts: the digits and the exponent of the last one.

python.Printing.zeros

Section titled “python.Printing.zeros”

Zeros, n of them.

python.Printing.exponent

Section titled “python.Printing.exponent”

The decimal exponent x with at least two digits and its sign (e+05, e-10).

python.Printing.layout

Section titled “python.Printing.layout”

The digits laid out with first, the power of ten of the first digit: fixed point when it is from -4 to 15, otherwise scientific.

python.Printing.float

Section titled “python.Printing.float”

repr of the float v.

python.Printing.escape

Section titled “python.Printing.escape”

The code point c in a str’s repr between the quote q: backslash, the quote and control characters escaped.

python.Printing.text

Section titled “python.Printing.text”

repr of the str cs: single quotes, or double quotes when it has a single quote and no double quote.

python.Printing.functionName

Section titled “python.Printing.functionName”

A function’s name as shown (up to the code point 1, which separates a nested function’s own name).

python.Printing.nameOf

Section titled “python.Printing.nameOf”

The name of the function value f (none for anything else).

python.Printing.range

Section titled “python.Printing.range”

repr of the range from start up to stop by step.

python.Printing.builtinName

Section titled “python.Printing.builtinName”

A built-in function’s name as its repr, __name__ and __qualname__ show it: without its module’s (math.sqrt is sqrt; the messages of a call name it with its module).

python.Printing.exceptionBase

Section titled “python.Printing.exceptionBase”

The built-in exception class the class c derives from (empty when none), looking up a user’s class on the heap h.

python.Printing.ownAttribute

Section titled “python.Printing.ownAttribute”

The attribute name among the attributes an object holds itself (not looked up through its class).

python.Printing.arguments

Section titled “python.Printing.arguments”

The arguments of the exception instance v on the heap h: its args attribute’s items.

python.Hidden

Section titled “python.Hidden”

What repr leaves out, to tell what its text depends on: nothing (the text itself), or objects’ addresses (the model’s allocation rule, which CPython’s memory does not follow).

python.Printing.addressOr

Section titled “python.Printing.addressOr”

The address the object at a shows, or nothing while hidden hides addresses.

python.Printing.function

Section titled “python.Printing.function”

repr of the function value named name with the identity of its object (none for a built-in): its address left out while hidden hides addresses.

python.Printing.wrapper

Section titled “python.Printing.wrapper”

repr of the staticmethod, classmethod or property object (its type name kind) at a, its address left out while hidden hides addresses.

python.Printing.reprWithin

Section titled “python.Printing.reprWithin”

repr of the value v on the heap h, leaving out what hidden hides, inside the containers at the addresses seen; fuel bounds the depth of references followed (a container inside itself is shown as such before it runs out).

python.Printing.reprAll

Section titled “python.Printing.reprAll”

repr of the values vs, each, with hidden, fuel, h and seen as for Printing.reprWithin.

python.Printing.tuple

Section titled “python.Printing.tuple”

repr of a tuple of items: a single item has a trailing comma (hidden, fuel, h and seen as for Printing.reprWithin).

python.Printing.pairs

Section titled “python.Printing.pairs”

repr of a dict’s pairs, each key: value (hidden, fuel, h and seen as for Printing.reprWithin).

python.Printing.object

Section titled “python.Printing.object”

repr of the object at address a on the heap h (hidden, fuel and seen as for Printing.reprWithin).

python.Printing.objectOf

Section titled “python.Printing.objectOf”

repr of the object o at address a; seen are the containers being shown around it (hidden, fuel and h as for Printing.reprWithin).

python.Printing.instance

Section titled “python.Printing.instance”

repr of the instance at address a of the class cls, with no __repr__: an exception’s class and arguments, else its class and address (hidden, fuel, h and seen as for Printing.reprWithin).

python.Printing.depth

Section titled “python.Printing.depth”

How deep repr may follow references: one more than the size of the heap h (each object is shown at most once on a path, so this is never reached by a finite value).

python.Printing.reprHiding

Section titled “python.Printing.reprHiding”

repr(v) on the heap h with what hidden hides left out.

python.Printing.repr

Section titled “python.Printing.repr”

repr(v), looking up objects on the heap h.

python.Printing.isKeyError

Section titled “python.Printing.isKeyError”

Whether the exception instance’s class cls (a user’s on the heap h) derives from KeyError.

python.Printing.exceptionText

Section titled “python.Printing.exceptionText”

str of an exception instance of the class cls from its arguments: none, the single argument’s str (KeyError’s repr), or the repr of the tuple of them; fuel bounds nested exceptions, h is the heap and what hidden hides is left out.

python.Printing.strWithin

Section titled “python.Printing.strWithin”

str(v) on the heap h within fuel nested exceptions, with what hidden hides left out.

python.Printing.strHiding

Section titled “python.Printing.strHiding”

str(v) on the heap h with what hidden hides left out.

python.Printing.str

Section titled “python.Printing.str”

str(v), looking up objects on the heap h.

python.Printing.reprShows

Section titled “python.Printing.reprShows”

Whether repr(v) on the heap h shows what hidden hides (its text differs without it).

python.Printing.strShows

Section titled “python.Printing.strShows”

Whether str(v) on the heap h shows what hidden hides (its text differs without it).

python.FormatSpec

Section titled “python.FormatSpec”

A format specification ([[fill]align][sign][#][0][width][,|_][.precision][type]), each mark as its code point (0 when absent): align one of <, >, ^, =; sign one of -, +, space; # the alternate form (a base’s prefix); grouping , or _.

python.FormatSpec.isAlign

Section titled “python.FormatSpec.isAlign”

Whether the code point c is an alignment mark.

python.FormatSpec.alignment

Section titled “python.FormatSpec.alignment”

The fill and alignment at the start of the specification text cs, and the rest.

python.FormatSpec.mark

Section titled “python.FormatSpec.mark”

The first code point of cs if it is one of the marks (else absent), and the rest.

python.FormatSpec.parse

Section titled “python.FormatSpec.parse”

The specification read from its text cs.

python.Printing.grouped

Section titled “python.Printing.grouped”

The digits with separator every three from the right.

python.Printing.fixed

Section titled “python.Printing.fixed”

The value n / d rounded to p decimal places (ties to even), written in fixed point.

python.Printing.scientific

Section titled “python.Printing.scientific”

The value n / d with p digits after the point in scientific notation (d.ddde+XX).

python.Printing.stripFraction

Section titled “python.Printing.stripFraction”

The number text cs with the trailing zeros of its fraction removed, and the point when nothing is left after it.

python.Printing.general

Section titled “python.Printing.general”

The general format (g) of n / d with p significant digits (the requested precision, 0 counting as 1): fixed point when the exponent is from -4 to below p (one less when there is no type and the point must stay, keepPoint), otherwise scientific; trailing zeros removed.

python.Printing.capitals

Section titled “python.Printing.capitals”

The code points cs in capitals (ASCII).

python.Printing.floatBody

Section titled “python.Printing.floatBody”

The body of the float x without its sign for a type and precision (x is not negative): f F e E g G %, or a precision alone (g keeping the point); NaN and infinity as CPython writes them.

python.Printing.signText

Section titled “python.Printing.signText”

The sign a number shows: - when negative, else as the specification spec asks.

python.Printing.padded

Section titled “python.Printing.padded”

The sign and body padded to the width of spec: aligned as it asks (numbers, rightByDefault, default to the right, text to the left; 0 without an alignment pads with zeros after the sign).

python.Printing.withGrouping

Section titled “python.Printing.withGrouping”

A number’s body with its integer digits grouped when the specification spec asks.

python.Printing.radix

Section titled “python.Printing.radix”

The natural n in base 2, 8 or 16 by the conversion type (b, o, x; X in capitals).

python.Printing.inBase

Section titled “python.Printing.inBase”

The digits of the natural n in base, within fuel digits (the digits so far, after, after it).

python.Printing.basePrefix

Section titled “python.Printing.basePrefix”

The prefix the alternate form (#) of spec gives an int in a base: 0x, 0X, 0o, 0b.

python.Printing.unknownCode

Section titled “python.Printing.unknownCode”

ValueError for a format type the value v cannot be formatted with (its type name looked up on the heap h).

python.Printing.produce

Section titled “python.Printing.produce”

The text cs as a result.

python.Printing.number

Section titled “python.Printing.number”

A number’s body with its sign, grouped and padded as the specification spec asks, as a str.

python.Printing.formatInt

Section titled “python.Printing.formatInt”

format(n, spec) of an int (shown as original in errors, its type name looked up on the heap h).

python.Printing.formatFloat

Section titled “python.Printing.formatFloat”

format(x, spec) of a float (the heap h for the errors’ type names).

python.Printing.formatText

Section titled “python.Printing.formatText”

format(s, spec) of the str cs: truncated to the precision, aligned left by default; grouping is an error (the heap h for the errors’ type names).

python.Printing.format

Section titled “python.Printing.format”

format(v, spec) with spec read from specText, looking up objects on the heap h: a bool with an empty specification is its str, otherwise an int; a value that is not a number or str only with an empty specification.

python.Printing.hasFlag

Section titled “python.Printing.hasFlag”

Whether the % flags have the flag c.

python.Printing.percentNumber

Section titled “python.Printing.percentNumber”

The int n for the conversion %x, %X or %o, padded to the digits width as printf-style flags ask (- left, 0 zeros after the sign, # the base’s prefix).

python.Printing.truncated

Section titled “python.Printing.truncated”

%d of the float x: its value truncated, or the error converting infinity or NaN.

python.Outcome.then

Section titled “python.Outcome.then”

The result of the outcome o given to the next step, or the exception passed on.

python.Printing.percentCharacter

Section titled “python.Printing.percentCharacter”

%c of the value v with the format specification spec: a str of length one, or an int as its code point (the heap h for the errors’ type names).

python.Printing.percentOne

Section titled “python.Printing.percentOne”

One % conversion of the value v on the heap h: its flags, width, precision and the conversion code.

python.PercentScan

Section titled “python.PercentScan”

What scanning a % format gives: the text so far and the values left, or an exception.

python.PercentScan.after

Section titled “python.PercentScan.after”

The text of one conversion’s outcome o given to next to add and go on scanning, or its exception.

python.PercentSpec

Section titled “python.PercentSpec”

The specification after a %: its flags, width, whether a precision is given and its digits, and the rest.

python.PercentSpec.read

Section titled “python.PercentSpec.read”

The specification read from cs, the text after a %.

python.Printing.percentWithin

Section titled “python.Printing.percentWithin”

Scanning the rest cs of a % format within fuel steps, looking up objects on the heap h: the text so far, the values left.

python.Printing.percent

Section titled “python.Printing.percent”

fmt % arg of the format text and the argument, looking up objects on the heap h: a tuple’s items one for each conversion, any other value as the only one.

python.Printing.digitLimit

Section titled “python.Printing.digitLimit”

The most decimal digits an int converts to or from (CPython 3.11’s default_max_str_digits).

python.Printing.tooLong

Section titled “python.Printing.tooLong”

Whether the int n has more decimal digits than the limit.

python.Printing.tooLongError

Section titled “python.Printing.tooLongError”

ValueError: an int too long to convert to decimal text.

python.Printing.objectTooLong

Section titled “python.Printing.objectTooLong”

Whether printing the object o on the heap h would convert an int too long (fuel: references still to follow).

python.Printing.holdsTooLong

Section titled “python.Printing.holdsTooLong”

Whether printing the value v on the heap h would convert an int too long (as repr, fuel bounds the references followed).

python.Printing.anyTooLong

Section titled “python.Printing.anyTooLong”

Whether printing any of the values vs on the heap h would convert an int too long, within fuel references.

python.Printing.checked

Section titled “python.Printing.checked”

ValueError when repr or str of the value v on the heap h would convert an int too long.
