formatting

cfamily.Flags

Section titled “cfamily.Flags”

The flags of a conversion: - (left), + (sign), space, # (alternative form), 0 (zeros).

cfamily.Flags.cleared

Section titled “cfamily.Flags.cleared”

No flags.

cfamily.Count

Section titled “cfamily.Count”

A width or a precision: not given, a number, or * (from an int argument).

cfamily.LengthModifier

Section titled “cfamily.LengthModifier”

A length modifier: none, hh, h, l, ll, z (size_t), j (intmax_t), t (ptrdiff_t).

cfamily.FloatStyle

Section titled “cfamily.FloatStyle”

How %f, %e, %g write a floating value.

cfamily.ConversionKind

Section titled “cfamily.ConversionKind”

What a conversion writes: d i, u, o, x X, c, s, f F e E g G.

cfamily.Conversion

Section titled “cfamily.Conversion”

A conversion specification.

cfamily.FormatPiece

Section titled “cfamily.FormatPiece”

A piece of a format: a character as is, %%, or a conversion.

cfamily.PrintArgument

Section titled “cfamily.PrintArgument”

An argument after the default argument promotions: an integer of its type, a string (its bytes up to the null, or up to a precision: none where reading them is undefined), or a double.

cfamily.Parsed

Section titled “cfamily.Parsed”

A value parsed from the front of a format, and the codes after it.

cfamily.Flags.add

Section titled “cfamily.Flags.add”

The flags with one more, if the code is a flag.

cfamily.Format.flags

Section titled “cfamily.Format.flags”

The flags at the front.

cfamily.Format.isDigit

Section titled “cfamily.Format.isDigit”

Whether a code is a decimal digit.

cfamily.Format.number

Section titled “cfamily.Format.number”

The decimal number at the front (acc so far).

cfamily.Format.givenCount

Section titled “cfamily.Format.givenCount”

A count from a number at the front.

cfamily.Format.width

Section titled “cfamily.Format.width”

The width: *, digits, or none.

cfamily.Format.precision

Section titled “cfamily.Format.precision”

The precision: . then * or digits (none is 0), or no precision.

cfamily.Format.doubled

Section titled “cfamily.Format.doubled”

The length after one h or l: doubled (hh, ll), or single.

cfamily.Format.lengthModifier

Section titled “cfamily.Format.lengthModifier”

The length modifier.

cfamily.Format.floatKind

Section titled “cfamily.Format.floatKind”

The floating conversion of a code: f F e E g G.

cfamily.Format.kind

Section titled “cfamily.Format.kind”

The conversion of a code.

cfamily.Conversion.isBare

Section titled “cfamily.Conversion.isBare”

Whether a conversion has nothing between % and its character (%% must be exactly that, C11 7.21.6.1p8).

cfamily.Format.parse

Section titled “cfamily.Format.parse”

The pieces of a format, none if it is not a valid format.

cfamily.LengthModifier.argumentType

Section titled “cfamily.LengthModifier.argumentType”

The type an integer conversion’s argument has after promotion (hh and h arguments were promoted to int).

cfamily.LengthModifier.writtenType

Section titled “cfamily.LengthModifier.writtenType”

The type an integer is converted back to before it is written (hh and h to the narrow type).

cfamily.ConversionKind.isSigned

Section titled “cfamily.ConversionKind.isSigned”

Whether a conversion is d or i.

cfamily.PrintArgument.integerAs

Section titled “cfamily.PrintArgument.integerAs”

The integer of an argument read as a type: the same type, or its signed or unsigned counterpart with the value in both ranges (C11 7.16.1.1p2).

cfamily.Conversion.flagsFit

Section titled “cfamily.Conversion.flagsFit”

Whether the flags and precision suit the conversion: # only with o x X and floating, no 0 or precision with c, no 0 with s (C11 7.21.6.1p6).

cfamily.Format.ofCodes

Section titled “cfamily.Format.ofCodes”

The characters of codes.

cfamily.Format.copies

Section titled “cfamily.Format.copies”

k copies of a character.

cfamily.Format.digit

Section titled “cfamily.Format.digit”

The digit of a value below 16 (letters upper or lower).

cfamily.Format.digitsIn

Section titled “cfamily.Format.digitsIn”

The digits of n in a base, most significant first (acc after them), within fuel.

cfamily.Format.inBase

Section titled “cfamily.Format.inBase”

n written in a base.

cfamily.Format.pad

Section titled “cfamily.Format.pad”

A field: the prefix and body padded to the width with spaces (left or right), or zeros after the prefix.

cfamily.Format.zeroPadded

Section titled “cfamily.Format.zeroPadded”

s padded on the left with zeros to n characters.

cfamily.ConversionKind.base

Section titled “cfamily.ConversionKind.base”

The base of an integer conversion.

cfamily.ConversionKind.upper

Section titled “cfamily.ConversionKind.upper”

Whether an integer conversion writes upper case.

cfamily.Format.integerDigits

Section titled “cfamily.Format.integerDigits”

An integer’s digits: the precision is the least count (default 1; precision 0 and value 0 write none); #o makes the first digit 0.

cfamily.Format.integerPrefix

Section titled “cfamily.Format.integerPrefix”

An integer’s prefix: its sign (d, i), or 0x for #x of a nonzero value.

cfamily.Format.integer

Section titled “cfamily.Format.integer”

An integer conversion of a value (already converted back to the written type).

cfamily.Format.exponent

Section titled “cfamily.Format.exponent”

“e+05”: the exponent with a sign and at least two digits.

cfamily.Format.fixed

Section titled “cfamily.Format.fixed”

%.pf of a finite value’s magnitude: the value rounded to p places, p digits after the point (none for p = 0).

cfamily.Format.scientific

Section titled “cfamily.Format.scientific”

%.pe of a finite value’s magnitude: p + 1 significant digits, one before the point.

cfamily.Format.trimmed

Section titled “cfamily.Format.trimmed”

The digits after the point with trailing zeros removed.

cfamily.Format.withPoint

Section titled “cfamily.Format.withPoint”

A whole part and a fraction: with the fraction’s trailing zeros removed (and the point if none remain), unless kept (#g).

cfamily.Format.general

Section titled “cfamily.Format.general”

%.pg of a finite nonzero magnitude with P significant digits (P = p, or 1 for 0): fixed if the exponent X has -4 <= X < P, else scientific; trailing zeros removed unless kept (#).

cfamily.Format.generalOf

Section titled “cfamily.Format.generalOf”

%.pg of a finite value’s magnitude.

cfamily.Format.pointAfterFirst

Section titled “cfamily.Format.pointAfterFirst”

Inserts the point after the first digit (“3e+00” to “3.e+00”).

cfamily.Format.magnitude

Section titled “cfamily.Format.magnitude”

A floating conversion of a finite value’s magnitude; # keeps the point at precision 0 (f, e).

cfamily.Format.special

Section titled “cfamily.Format.special”

inf or nan, upper case for F E G.

cfamily.Format.isNegative

Section titled “cfamily.Format.isNegative”

Whether a floating value is negative (NaN has no sign here).

cfamily.Format.isFinite

Section titled “cfamily.Format.isFinite”

Whether a floating value is finite.

cfamily.Format.float

Section titled “cfamily.Format.float”

A floating conversion: the sign apart (0 pads after it; inf and nan are not zero padded; NaN takes no + or space, as clang’s libc).

cfamily.Fields

Section titled “cfamily.Fields”

The width, the precision and the arguments left, after any * took an int argument (a negative width is the - flag, a negative precision is none).

cfamily.Fields.of

Section titled “cfamily.Fields.of”

Fields with a width and no precision yet.

cfamily.Fields.withPrecision

Section titled “cfamily.Fields.withPrecision”

The fields with a precision and the arguments after it.

cfamily.Fields.width

Section titled “cfamily.Fields.width”

The width of a conversion.

cfamily.Fields.precision

Section titled “cfamily.Fields.precision”

The precision of a conversion, after its width.

cfamily.Written

Section titled “cfamily.Written”

What a conversion writes, and the arguments after it.

cfamily.Format.integerArgument

Section titled “cfamily.Format.integerArgument”

An integer or character conversion of an argument.

cfamily.Format.textArgument

Section titled “cfamily.Format.textArgument”

A string conversion: no length modifier (%ls is not in the subset), bytes read up to the precision.

cfamily.Format.floatArgument

Section titled “cfamily.Format.floatArgument”

A floating conversion: no length or l (%lf is %f); L (long double) is not in the subset.

cfamily.Format.convert

Section titled “cfamily.Format.convert”

A conversion of the next argument.

cfamily.Format.write

Section titled “cfamily.Format.write”

The pieces written with the arguments.

cfamily.Format.print

Section titled “cfamily.Format.print”

printf’s output for a format and its arguments; none where the behavior is undefined (C11 7.21.6.1p9, and an output longer than INT_MAX, 7.21.6.1p15).
