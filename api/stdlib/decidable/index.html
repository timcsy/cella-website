decidable

decToBoolYes

Section titled “decToBoolYes”

A positive decision yes p converts to the boolean true.

decToBoolNo

Section titled “decToBoolNo”

A negative decision no f converts to the boolean false.

decMap

Section titled “decMap”

Transports a decision for P to a decision for Q along maps in both directions.

decMapYes

Section titled “decMapYes”

Mapping a positive decision gives yes of the mapped witness.

decMapNo

Section titled “decMapNo”

Mapping a negative decision gives no with the refutation precomposed by the backward map.

decNot

Section titled “decNot”

Decides the negation of P from a decision for P.

decAnd

Section titled “decAnd”

Decides the conjunction (product) of two decidable propositions.

decOr

Section titled “decOr”

Decides the disjunction (Either) of two decidable propositions, preferring the left one.

decImpl

Section titled “decImpl”

Decides the implication P -> Q from decisions for P and Q.

decEquiv

Section titled “decEquiv”

Transports a decision across a logical equivalence given by maps both ways; an alias of decMap.

decRefl

Section titled “decRefl”

Decides a = a positively by reflexivity.

decUnit

Section titled “decUnit”

The decision that Unit holds.

decEmpty

Section titled “decEmpty”

The decision that Empty does not hold.

decNotYes

Section titled “decNotYes”

Negating a positive decision yields boolean false.

decNotNo

Section titled “decNotNo”

Negating a negative decision yields boolean true.

decNotNot

Section titled “decNotNot”

Double negation of a decision has the same boolean value as the original.

decAndYesYes

Section titled “decAndYesYes”

The conjunction of two positive decisions is true.

decAndYesNo

Section titled “decAndYesNo”

The conjunction of a positive and a negative decision is false.

decAndNoYes

Section titled “decAndNoYes”

The conjunction of a negative and a positive decision is false.

decAndNoNo

Section titled “decAndNoNo”

The conjunction of two negative decisions is false.

decAndBool

Section titled “decAndBool”

The boolean value of decAnd is the boolean and of the two decisions’ values.

decOrYesYes

Section titled “decOrYesYes”

The disjunction of two positive decisions is true.

decOrYesNo

Section titled “decOrYesNo”

The disjunction of a positive and a negative decision is true.

decOrNoYes

Section titled “decOrNoYes”

The disjunction of a negative and a positive decision is true.

decOrNoNo

Section titled “decOrNoNo”

The disjunction of two negative decisions is false.

decOrBool

Section titled “decOrBool”

The boolean value of decOr is the boolean or of the two decisions’ values.

decImplNoAny

Section titled “decImplNoAny”

An implication with a refuted premise is decided true, whatever the conclusion.

decImplYesYes

Section titled “decImplYesYes”

An implication between two positive decisions is true.

decImplYesNo

Section titled “decImplYesNo”

An implication from a positive to a negative decision is false.

decEquivYes

Section titled “decEquivYes”

Transporting a positive decision with decEquiv stays true.

decEquivNo

Section titled “decEquivNo”

Transporting a negative decision with decEquiv stays false.

decReflBool

Section titled “decReflBool”

The reflexivity decision decRefl a is true.

decAndDecNotYes

Section titled “decAndDecNotYes”

A positive decision conjoined with its own negation is false.

decAndDecNotNo

Section titled “decAndDecNotNo”

A negative decision conjoined with its own negation is false.

decOrDecNotYes

Section titled “decOrDecNotYes”

A positive decision disjoined with its own negation is true.

decOrDecNotNo

Section titled “decOrDecNotNo”

A negative decision disjoined with its own negation is true.

decExcludedMiddle

Section titled “decExcludedMiddle”

Excluded middle for decisions: a decision or its negation is always true.

decAndCommBool

Section titled “decAndCommBool”

Conjunction of decisions is commutative at the boolean level.

decOrCommBool

Section titled “decOrCommBool”

Disjunction of decisions is commutative at the boolean level.

decContra

Section titled “decContra”

Decides the negation of P (contrapositive form); the same as decNot.

decId

Section titled “decId”

The identity on decisions.

decConstYes

Section titled “decConstYes”

The constant positive decision built from a witness.

decConstNo

Section titled “decConstNo”

The constant negative decision built from a refutation.

decAndAssocBool

Section titled “decAndAssocBool”

Conjunction of decisions is associative at the boolean level.

decOrAssocBool

Section titled “decOrAssocBool”

Disjunction of decisions is associative at the boolean level.

decAndUnitL

Section titled “decAndUnitL”

decUnit is a left unit for decAnd at the boolean level.

decAndUnitR

Section titled “decAndUnitR”

decUnit is a right unit for decAnd at the boolean level.

decOrEmptyL

Section titled “decOrEmptyL”

decEmpty is a left unit for decOr at the boolean level.

decOrEmptyR

Section titled “decOrEmptyR”

decEmpty is a right unit for decOr at the boolean level.

decAndEmptyL

Section titled “decAndEmptyL”

decEmpty annihilates decAnd on the left: the result is false.

decOrUnitL

Section titled “decOrUnitL”

decUnit annihilates decOr on the left: the result is true.

decDeMorganAndBool

Section titled “decDeMorganAndBool”

De Morgan at the boolean level: the negation of a conjunction is the disjunction of the negations.

decDeMorganOrBool

Section titled “decDeMorganOrBool”

De Morgan at the boolean level: the negation of a disjunction is the conjunction of the negations.

decIdBool

Section titled “decIdBool”

decId leaves the boolean value of a decision unchanged.

decContraBool

Section titled “decContraBool”

decContra has the same boolean value as decNot.

decConstYesBool

Section titled “decConstYesBool”

decConstYes is always true.

decConstNoBool

Section titled “decConstNoBool”

decConstNo is always false.

decAndOrDistribBool

Section titled “decAndOrDistribBool”

Conjunction distributes over disjunction of decisions at the boolean level.

decAndIdempotent

Section titled “decAndIdempotent”

Conjunction of a decision with itself has the same boolean value as the decision.

decOrIdempotent

Section titled “decOrIdempotent”

Disjunction of a decision with itself has the same boolean value as the decision.

decAndAbsorb

Section titled “decAndAbsorb”

Absorption: dp conjoined with dp or dq has the boolean value of dp.

decOrAbsorb

Section titled “decOrAbsorb”

Absorption: dp disjoined with dp and dq has the boolean value of dp.

decAndComplement

Section titled “decAndComplement”

A decision conjoined with its negation is always false.

decIff

Section titled “decIff”

Decides the biconditional (maps both ways) of two decidable propositions.

decXorBool

Section titled “decXorBool”

Boolean exclusive or of two decisions.

decNandBool

Section titled “decNandBool”

Boolean not-and of two decisions.

decNorBool

Section titled “decNorBool”

Boolean not-or of two decisions.

decIffYesYes

Section titled “decIffYesYes”

The biconditional of two positive decisions is true.

decIffNoNo

Section titled “decIffNoNo”

The biconditional of two negative decisions is true.

decIffYesNo

Section titled “decIffYesNo”

The biconditional of a positive and a negative decision is false.

decIffNoYes

Section titled “decIffNoYes”

The biconditional of a negative and a positive decision is false.

decIffRefl

Section titled “decIffRefl”

The biconditional of a decision with itself is true.

decIffSymm

Section titled “decIffSymm”

The biconditional of decisions is symmetric at the boolean level.

decXorBoolYesYes

Section titled “decXorBoolYesYes”

Exclusive or of two positive decisions is false.

decXorBoolYesNo

Section titled “decXorBoolYesNo”

Exclusive or of a positive and a negative decision is true.

decXorBoolNoYes

Section titled “decXorBoolNoYes”

Exclusive or of a negative and a positive decision is true.

decXorBoolNoNo

Section titled “decXorBoolNoNo”

Exclusive or of two negative decisions is false.

decXorComm

Section titled “decXorComm”

Exclusive or of decisions is commutative.

decNandBoolYesYes

Section titled “decNandBoolYesYes”

Not-and of two positive decisions is false.

decNandBoolYesNo

Section titled “decNandBoolYesNo”

Not-and of a positive and a negative decision is true.

decNandBoolNoYes

Section titled “decNandBoolNoYes”

Not-and of a negative and a positive decision is true.

decNandBoolNoNo

Section titled “decNandBoolNoNo”

Not-and of two negative decisions is true.

decNorBoolYesYes

Section titled “decNorBoolYesYes”

Not-or of two positive decisions is false.

decNorBoolYesNo

Section titled “decNorBoolYesNo”

Not-or of a positive and a negative decision is false.

decNorBoolNoYes

Section titled “decNorBoolNoYes”

Not-or of a negative and a positive decision is false.

decNorBoolNoNo

Section titled “decNorBoolNoNo”

Not-or of two negative decisions is true.

decJoin

Section titled “decJoin”

Flattens a decision about a decision into a decision about P.

decJoinYesYes

Section titled “decJoinYesYes”

Joining yes (yes p) gives yes p.

decJoinYesNo

Section titled “decJoinYesNo”

Joining yes (no f) gives no f.

decDNE

Section titled “decDNE”

Double negation elimination for a decidable proposition.

decEqProd

Section titled “decEqProd”

Decides a product from decisions for its components; an alias of decAnd.

decEqProdYY

Section titled “decEqProdYY”

decEqProd of two positive decisions is true.

decEqProdYN

Section titled “decEqProdYN”

decEqProd of a positive and a negative decision is false.

decEqProdNY

Section titled “decEqProdNY”

decEqProd of a negative and a positive decision is false.

decEqProdNN

Section titled “decEqProdNN”

decEqProd of two negative decisions is false.

decEqSum

Section titled “decEqSum”

Decides a sum (Either) from decisions for its components; an alias of decOr.

decEqSumYY

Section titled “decEqSumYY”

decEqSum of two positive decisions is true.

decEqSumYN

Section titled “decEqSumYN”

decEqSum of a positive and a negative decision is true.

decEqSumNY

Section titled “decEqSumNY”

decEqSum of a negative and a positive decision is true.

decEqSumNN

Section titled “decEqSumNN”

decEqSum of two negative decisions is false.

decTrue

Section titled “decTrue”

Decides true = true positively by reflexivity.

decElim

Section titled “decElim”

Eliminates a decision into any type by handling the positive and negative cases.

decElimYes

Section titled “decElimYes”

Eliminating a positive decision runs the yes branch on the witness.

decElimNo

Section titled “decElimNo”

Eliminating a negative decision runs the no branch on the refutation.

decOrAndDistribBool

Section titled “decOrAndDistribBool”

Disjunction distributes over conjunction of decisions at the boolean level.

decImplRefl

Section titled “decImplRefl”

The implication from a decision to itself is true.

decTripleNot

Section titled “decTripleNot”

Triple negation of a decision has the same boolean value as single negation.

decXorSelf

Section titled “decXorSelf”

Exclusive or of a decision with itself is false.

decNandSelf

Section titled “decNandSelf”

Not-and of a decision with itself is the boolean negation of the decision.

decNorSelf

Section titled “decNorSelf”

Not-or of a decision with itself is the boolean negation of the decision.

decWhen

Section titled “decWhen”

Applies a function to the witness of a positive decision, or returns a default when it is negative.

decWhenYes

Section titled “decWhenYes”

decWhen on a positive decision applies the function to the witness.

decWhenNo

Section titled “decWhenNo”

decWhen on a negative decision returns the default.

Decidable

Section titled “Decidable”

The class of decidable propositions, carrying a decision dec; instances drive the decide tactic.

ofDecideTrue

Section titled “ofDecideTrue”

Extracts a proof of P from a decision whose boolean value computes to true.

ofDecide

Section titled “ofDecide”

Proof produced by decide: a proof of P from the instance’s decision computing to true.

decEqBool

Section titled “decEqBool”

a = b on booleans is decidable (used by decide).

zeroNeqSuccD

Section titled “zeroNeqSuccD”

Zero is not the successor of any natural number.

succNeqZeroD

Section titled “succNeqZeroD”

A successor is never zero.

natDecEq

Section titled “natDecEq”

Decidable equality of natural numbers.

decEqNat

Section titled “decEqNat”

m = n on naturals is decidable (used by decide).
