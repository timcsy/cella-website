effects

Effects

Section titled “Effects”

A set of effects. wait: waits for the outside world. net, fs, clock, random, proc, pin, env, cwd: touches the network, files, the clock, random numbers, external programs, hardware pins, environment variables, the current directory. race, event: the result depends on which of several things finishes first, or on outside events. console: prints. debug: has debug output.

Effects.none

Section titled “Effects.none”

No effects.

Effects.join

Section titled “Effects.join”

The union of the sets of effects a and b.

Effects.sub

Section titled “Effects.sub”

Whether every effect in a is in b.

Effects.below

Section titled “Effects.below”

One field of the inclusion: x implies y, and the remaining fields rest hold.

Effects.impliesTrans

Section titled “Effects.impliesTrans”

Implication of Booleans is transitive: x implies y and y implies z give x implies z.

Effects.belowTrans

Section titled “Effects.belowTrans”

One field of the inclusion is transitive (p for x to y, q for y to z), given rest: the remaining fields are.

Effects.subTrans

Section titled “Effects.subTrans”

Inclusion is transitive: a in b (p) and b in c (q) give a in c.

Effects.impliesOr

Section titled “Effects.impliesOr”

x or y implies z when x does (p) and y does (q).

Effects.belowJoin

Section titled “Effects.belowJoin”

One field of the inclusion of a union (p for x to z, q for y to z), given rest: the remaining fields are.

Effects.joinSub

Section titled “Effects.joinSub”

The union of a and b is in c when a is (p) and b is (q).

Effects.impliesOrBoth

Section titled “Effects.impliesOrBoth”

x or y implies x or z when y implies z (p).

Effects.belowJoinBoth

Section titled “Effects.belowJoinBoth”

One field of the inclusion of two unions with x (p for y to z), given rest: the remaining fields are.

Effects.joinSubJoin

Section titled “Effects.joinSubJoin”

Adding a to both sides keeps an inclusion: b in c (p) gives the union of a and b in the union of a and c.

Effects.impliesOfOr

Section titled “Effects.impliesOfOr”

y implies x or y.

Effects.belowOfJoin

Section titled “Effects.belowOfJoin”

One field of the inclusion of a set in its union with another (y in x or y), given rest: the remaining fields are.

Effects.subOfJoin

Section titled “Effects.subOfJoin”

b is in the union of a and b.

Duration

Section titled “Duration”

A length of time, in milliseconds.

Duration.millis

Section titled “Duration.millis”

n milliseconds.

Duration.seconds

Section titled “Duration.seconds”

n seconds.

Duration.minutes

Section titled “Duration.minutes”

n minutes.

Duration.add

Section titled “Duration.add”

The two lengths one after the other: d1 + d2.

Duration.lt

Section titled “Duration.lt”

Whether d1 is shorter than d2.

Duration.le

Section titled “Duration.le”

Whether d1 is at most as long as d2.

Request

Section titled “Request”

A request to the outside world. allOf(rs) sends the requests rs together and answers each; within(d, r) answers r when it comes within the time d; firstOf(rs) sends the requests rs together and answers the first to finish.

Effects.touching

Section titled “Effects.touching”

The set with only the given effects among waiting and the kinds of the outside world: wait, net, fs, clock, random, proc, pin as given, the others none.

Effects.reading

Section titled “Effects.reading”

The set with only reading environment variables (env) or the current directory (cwd) as given.

Effects.output

Section titled “Effects.output”

The set with only output (console): what printing needs.

Request.needs

Section titled “Request.needs”

The effects the request r needs.

Request.needsAll

Section titled “Request.needsAll”

The effects the requests rs need together.

Request.neverPure

Section titled “Request.neverPure”

Every request needs some effect, so no request is allowed with none: a proof ok that the request r is allowed with no effects is impossible.

Response

Section titled “Response”

What the outside world answers to the request r. A request that can fail answers err with why it failed; one with a time limit answers none when the time runs out.

Response.ofAll

Section titled “Response.ofAll”

What the outside world answers to all of the requests rs: the answer to each, in order.

Response.ofFirst

Section titled “Response.ofFirst”

What the outside world answers to the first of the requests rs to finish: left with the answer to the first request, or right with the answer to one of the others.

Scope

Section titled “Scope”

A scope that computations wait in. No program makes one yet.

Eff

Section titled “Eff”

A computation with effects e, waiting in the scopes ss, that gives an A: it has finished with a, or it sends the request r, which ok proves e allows, and continues with k on the response.

Eff.pure

Section titled “Eff.pure”

The computation that gives a with no request.

Eff.bind

Section titled “Eff.bind”

m, then f on its result.

Eff.widen

Section titled “Eff.widen”

m as a computation with the larger set of effects e2, which p proves includes e1.

Eff.runPure

Section titled “Eff.runPure”

The value of the computation m with no effects: it sends no request.

Eff.bindPure

Section titled “Eff.bindPure”

Binding the finished computation pure(a) to f applies f to a.

Eff.bindIntoPure

Section titled “Eff.bindIntoPure”

Binding m with pure changes nothing.

Eff.bindAssociative

Section titled “Eff.bindAssociative”

Binding is associative: m, then f, then g, grouped either way.

Task.allEffects

Section titled “Task.allEffects”

What Task.all adds to the effects of the computations it runs: it waits.

Task.timeoutEffects

Section titled “Task.timeoutEffects”

What Task.timeout adds to the effects of the computation it runs: it waits and reads the clock.

Task.raceEffects

Section titled “Task.raceEffects”

What Task.race adds to the effects of the computations it runs: it waits, and which one finishes first is not determined.

Task.timedOut

Section titled “Task.timedOut”

The kind of the error of a computation stopped by Task.timeout because its time ran out.

Task.timedOutError

Section titled “Task.timedOutError”

The error of a computation stopped by Task.timeout because its time ran out, made at at: the message timed out, the kind Task.timedOut.

Task.pending

Section titled “Task.pending”

The requests the computations ms are waiting on, in order: one for each that has not finished.

Task.needs

Section titled “Task.needs”

What the requests the computations ms are waiting on need.

Task.Needed

Section titled “Task.Needed”

That e allows every request the computations ms are waiting on.

Task.pendingSub

Section titled “Task.pendingSub”

Every request the computations ms are waiting on is allowed by e.

Task.lifted

Section titled “Task.lifted”

The computation m as one with the larger set of effects Effects.join(Task.allEffects, e).

Task.Results

Section titled “Task.Results”

The results of several computations that may fail, each an A: all of them in order, or the first error.

Task.onto

Section titled “Task.onto”

The result x in front of the results r, or the error of r.

Task.before

Section titled “Task.before”

The result r in front of the results xs, or the error of r.

Task.prepend

Section titled “Task.prepend”

The computation n with the result x put in front of its results.

Task.append

Section titled “Task.append”

The computation m with its result put in front of the results xs.

Task.pairSub

Section titled “Task.pairSub”

That e allows the requests r1 and r2 sent together, from ok1 and ok2.

Task.both

Section titled “Task.both”

The computations m and n run at the same time: while both wait, their two requests are sent at once and each continues with its answer. It gives the result of m in front of those of n once both have finished, or the error of either as soon as it fails; the other is not continued.

Task.all

Section titled “Task.all”

f on each element of xs, all at the same time: the results in the order of xs once all have finished, or the first error as soon as one fails (the others are stopped).

Task.runBefore

Section titled “Task.runBefore”

The computation m stopped at the time end (in milliseconds of the clock): each request it sends is given the time left, and when none is left it gives the error Task.timedOutError(at).

Task.timeout

Section titled “Task.timeout”

The computation m, given the time d: its result when it finishes in time, otherwise an error of the kind Task.timedOut made at at (the place of the call when not given); what it was waiting on is stopped.

Task.Answer

Section titled “Task.Answer”

What the outside world answers to the first of the requests the computations ms are waiting on.

Task.anyDone

Section titled “Task.anyDone”

The result of one of the computations ms that has finished.

Task.firstAnswer

Section titled “Task.firstAnswer”

The computation among ms whose request was answered first, v, continued with its answer.

Task.race

Section titled “Task.race”

The first of the computations ms to finish, whether it succeeds or fails; the others are stopped. One that has already finished wins; otherwise the request of each is sent at once, and the one answered first continues alone. Which one that is depends on timing, so the effects have race.

Net

Section titled “Net”

The network, as a capability: a function that touches the network takes one with given (net: Net). The runtime gives main the real one.

Fs

Section titled “Fs”

Files, as a capability (given (fs: Fs)).

Clock

Section titled “Clock”

The clock, as a capability (given (clock: Clock)).

Random

Section titled “Random”

Random numbers, as a capability (given (random: Random)).

Proc

Section titled “Proc”

External programs, as a capability (given (proc: Proc)).

Pin

Section titled “Pin”

Hardware pins, as a capability (given (pin: Pin)).

Env

Section titled “Env”

Environment variables, as a capability (given (env: Env)).

Cwd

Section titled “Cwd”

The current directory, as a capability (given (cwd: Cwd)).

Net.real

Section titled “Net.real”

The network the runtime gives to a main that takes given (net: Net) (cella run, the browser).

Fs.real

Section titled “Fs.real”

The files the runtime gives (the browser cannot answer for them).

Clock.real

Section titled “Clock.real”

The clock the runtime gives.

Random.real

Section titled “Random.real”

The random numbers the runtime gives (the command line cannot answer for them yet).

Proc.real

Section titled “Proc.real”

The external programs the runtime gives (the browser cannot answer for them).

Pin.real

Section titled “Pin.real”

The hardware pins the runtime gives (neither the command line nor the browser can answer for them yet).

Env.real

Section titled “Env.real”

The environment variables the runtime gives (the browser has none).

Cwd.real

Section titled “Cwd.real”

The current directory the runtime gives (the browser cannot answer for it).

Request.result

Section titled “Request.result”

The answer r of a request that can fail, with its error put at at, the place of the operation that sent it.

Net.get

Section titled “Net.get”

The page at url, fetched with the network net, or why it could not be fetched (an error made where it is called).

Net.post

Section titled “Net.post”

The answer of url to the text body sent to it over the network net, or why it could not be sent.

Fs.read

Section titled “Fs.read”

The contents of the file at path, read with the files fs, or why it could not be read.

Fs.write

Section titled “Fs.write”

Writes text to the file at path with the files fs (replacing what was there), or gives why it could not.

Fs.list

Section titled “Fs.list”

The names in the directory dir, read with the files fs, in dictionary order, or why it could not be read.

Clock.now

Section titled “Clock.now”

The time on the clock, in milliseconds.

Clock.sleep

Section titled “Clock.sleep”

Waits for the time d on the clock clock.

Random.below

Section titled “Random.below”

A random number below bound.

Proc.run

Section titled “Proc.run”

Runs program with the external programs proc, the arguments args and the input stdin, and waits: its exit code, output and error output, or why it could not be run.

Pin.read

Section titled “Pin.read”

Whether the pin at is high.

Pin.write

Section titled “Pin.write”

Sets the pin at high or low.

Env.get

Section titled “Env.get”

The value of the environment variable name, read with env, if it is set.

Cwd.get

Section titled “Cwd.get”

The current directory, read with cwd.

print

Section titled “print”

Prints the line text. Printing is an effect, not a capability: it is called directly, with no given.

printError

Section titled “printError”

Prints the line text to the error output.

Request.takesTime

Section titled “Request.takesTime”

Whether answering the request r takes time: the network, an external program, waiting.

Request.words

Section titled “Request.words”

The words that ask a runtime answering by text for the answer to the request r: its kind, then its arguments. The requests made of others (allOf, within, firstOf) are asked through their parts (Request.answerByText).

Response.failed

Section titled “Response.failed”

The error a request failed with, from the reason reason the outside world gave: not classified (ErrorKind.other), with no known place (the operation that sent the request puts in its own, Request.result).

Response.fromEither

Section titled “Response.fromEither”

The answer r of the outside world that may fail, with the reason it failed as an error (Response.failed).

Response.readResult

Section titled “Response.readResult”

The answer that may fail written as the text t: + and the value, or - and the reason it failed.

Response.readProgram

Section titled “Response.readProgram”

The answer of an external program written as the text t: +, its exit code, a newline, the length of its output, a newline, its output and its error output; or - and the reason it could not run.

Response.readNames

Section titled “Response.readNames”

The names in a directory written as the text t, one on each line; none written as the empty text.

Response.readBool

Section titled “Response.readBool”

A truth value written as the text t: 1 for true and 0 for false; none when t is neither.

Response.readOption

Section titled “Response.readOption”

A value that may be missing written as the text t: + and the value, or - when it is missing.

Response.read

Section titled “Response.read”

The answer to the request r read from the text t a runtime answered it with; none when t is not one.

Response.firstFinished

Section titled “Response.firstFinished”

The answer to the requests cons(r, rest) when the first of them, r, finished first with v.

Response.otherFinished

Section titled “Response.otherFinished”

The answer to the requests cons(r, rest) when one of the others, rest, finished first with w.

Request.Answer

Section titled “Request.Answer”

The answer to the request r in the kind of action M, when it can be read.

Request.mapAnswer

Section titled “Request.mapAnswer”

The result of the action m changed by f, in the kind of action M (pure, bind).

Request.answerByText

Section titled “Request.answerByText”

The answer to the request r from a runtime that answers by text, in its kind of action M (pure, bind): ask sends it the words of a request and gives the text it answers. The requests sent together (allOf) are asked one after another; one with a time limit (within) is asked with the limit when it is a single request; of several requests sent to race (firstOf) one that takes no time is answered at once, otherwise the runtime races them. None when an answer cannot be read.

Request.askByText

Section titled “Request.askByText”

The answer to the single request r, in the kind of action M (pure, bind), asked with its words by ask.

Request.answerAllByText

Section titled “Request.answerAllByText”

The answers to the requests rs, in the kind of action M (pure, bind), asked by ask one after another.

Request.inTime

Section titled “Request.inTime”

The answer the action m gives in the kind of action M (pure, bind), as one that came in time.

Request.askWithin

Section titled “Request.askWithin”

The answer to the single request r asked by ask with the time limit d, in the kind of action M (pure, bind): ~ is the answer when the time runs out.

Request.answerWithinByText

Section titled “Request.answerWithinByText”

The answer to the request q when it comes within the time d, in the kind of action M (pure, bind): a single request is asked by ask with the limit; one made of others is answered as it is.

Request.answerFirstAtOnceByText

Section titled “Request.answerFirstAtOnceByText”

The answer to the first of the requests rs that takes no time, in the kind of action M (pure, bind), asked by ask at once. Asked only when one of them takes no time: none when they all take time.

Request.raceWords

Section titled “Request.raceWords”

The words that ask a runtime to race the requests rs: for each, the number of its words and the words.

Response.readFirst

Section titled “Response.readFirst”

The answer to the requests rs when the one at position i answered t first.

Response.readRace

Section titled “Response.readRace”

The answer to the requests rs from the text t a runtime raced them with: the position of the first to finish, a newline and its answer.

Request.answerFirstByText

Section titled “Request.answerFirstByText”

The answer to the first of the requests rs to finish, in the kind of action M (pure, bind), asked by ask: one that takes no time is answered at once; otherwise the runtime races them.

Eff.runByText

Section titled “Eff.runByText”

Runs the computation m on a runtime that answers requests by text, in its kind of action M (pure, bind): ask sends it the words of each request (Request.answerByText) and gives the text it answers. When an answer cannot be read, it asks the runtime to stop with the reason and gives none. The browser runs a main with effects this way.
