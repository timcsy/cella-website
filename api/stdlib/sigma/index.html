sigma

sigmaEtaNonDep

Section titled “sigmaEtaNonDep”

Eta for non-dependent pairs: rebuilding a pair from its projections gives the same pair.

fstBeta

Section titled “fstBeta”

Beta for the first projection: fst (a, b) is a.

sndBeta

Section titled “sndBeta”

Beta for the second projection: snd (a, b) is b.

mkPair

Section titled “mkPair”

Builds a dependent pair from a base point and an element of its fiber.

mkPairNonDep

Section titled “mkPairNonDep”

Builds a non-dependent pair from two values.

sigmaPathNonDep

Section titled “sigmaPathNonDep”

A path in A × B built from a path between the first components and a path between the second components.

fstPathSigma

Section titled “fstPathSigma”

The base path of a path in a dependent sum, obtained by projecting the first component.

fstPathNonDep

Section titled “fstPathNonDep”

The path between first components obtained from a path in A × B.

sndPathNonDep

Section titled “sndPathNonDep”

The path between second components obtained from a path in A × B.

sigmaMap

Section titled “sigmaMap”

Maps a dependent pair along a base function and a fiberwise function over it.

sigmaMapFstNonDep

Section titled “sigmaMapFstNonDep”

Applies a function to the first component of a non-dependent pair.

sigmaMapNonDepComp

Section titled “sigmaMapNonDepComp”

Reflexivity at the pair obtained by applying two composed functions componentwise (a trivial statement).

congPairNonDep

Section titled “congPairNonDep”

Paths between the components give a path between the pairs.

fiberMk

Section titled “fiberMk”

Builds an element of the fiber of f over b from a point and a path f a = b.

fiberPoint

Section titled “fiberPoint”

The point of an element of a fiber.

fiberPath

Section titled “fiberPath”

The path f a = b carried by an element of a fiber.

fiberAtPoint

Section titled “fiberAtPoint”

Every point a lies in the fiber of f over f a, via the constant path.

fiberMapId

Section titled “fiberMapId”

The identity map on a fiber.

transportSigmaProdConst

Section titled “transportSigmaProdConst”

Transport along the constant family at A × B is the identity.

transportSndConst

Section titled “transportSndConst”

Transport along the constant family at A × B leaves the second component unchanged.

isPropProd2

Section titled “isPropProd2”

The product of two propositions is a proposition.

isPropSigmaUnit

Section titled “isPropSigmaUnit”

A × Unit is a proposition when A is.

sigmaAssocFwd

Section titled “sigmaAssocFwd”

Reassociates a left-nested triple (A × B) × C into the right-nested A × (B × C).

sigmaAssocBwd

Section titled “sigmaAssocBwd”

Reassociates a right-nested triple A × (B × C) into the left-nested (A × B) × C.

sigmaAssocFwdBwd

Section titled “sigmaAssocFwdBwd”

Reassociating forward and back after sigmaAssocBwd returns the same left-nested triple.

sigmaAssocBwdFwd

Section titled “sigmaAssocBwdFwd”

Reassociating back and forward after sigmaAssocFwd returns the same right-nested triple.

sigmaAssocRoundtrip1

Section titled “sigmaAssocRoundtrip1”

sigmaAssocFwd after sigmaAssocBwd is the identity on right-nested triples.

sigmaAssocRoundtrip2

Section titled “sigmaAssocRoundtrip2”

sigmaAssocBwd after sigmaAssocFwd is the identity on left-nested triples.

sigmaAssocFwdCompute

Section titled “sigmaAssocFwdCompute”

sigmaAssocFwd ((a, b), c) computes to (a, (b, c)).

sigmaAssocBwdCompute

Section titled “sigmaAssocBwdCompute”

sigmaAssocBwd (a, (b, c)) computes to ((a, b), c).

sigmaAssocDep3Fwd

Section titled “sigmaAssocDep3Fwd”

Reassociates a pair of pairs (A × B) × (C × D) into the right-nested A × (B × (C × D)).

sigmaAssocDep3Bwd

Section titled “sigmaAssocDep3Bwd”

Reassociates a right-nested quadruple A × (B × (C × D)) into the pair of pairs (A × B) × (C × D).

sigmaAssocDep3Roundtrip1

Section titled “sigmaAssocDep3Roundtrip1”

sigmaAssocDep3Fwd after sigmaAssocDep3Bwd is the identity on right-nested quadruples.

sigmaAssocDep3Roundtrip2

Section titled “sigmaAssocDep3Roundtrip2”

sigmaAssocDep3Bwd after sigmaAssocDep3Fwd is the identity on pairs of pairs.

sigmaSwap

Section titled “sigmaSwap”

Swaps the two components of a pair.

sigmaSwapSwap

Section titled “sigmaSwapSwap”

Swapping a pair twice gives back the original pair.

sigmaSwapCompute

Section titled “sigmaSwapCompute”

sigmaSwap (a, b) computes to (b, a).

sigmaSwapFst

Section titled “sigmaSwapFst”

The first component of a swapped pair is the original second component.

sigmaSwapSnd

Section titled “sigmaSwapSnd”

The second component of a swapped pair is the original first component.

sigmaSwapPath

Section titled “sigmaSwapPath”

Swapping is functorial on paths: a path between pairs gives a path between the swapped pairs.

sigmaUnitFwd

Section titled “sigmaUnitFwd”

Forward map of A × Unit ≅ A: drops the unit component.

sigmaUnitBwd

Section titled “sigmaUnitBwd”

Backward map of A × Unit ≅ A: pairs a value with unit.

sigmaUnitRoundtrip1

Section titled “sigmaUnitRoundtrip1”

Pairing with unit and dropping it again gives back the value.

sigmaUnitRoundtrip2

Section titled “sigmaUnitRoundtrip2”

Dropping the unit component and pairing it back gives the original pair.

unitSigmaFwd

Section titled “unitSigmaFwd”

Forward map of Unit × A ≅ A: drops the unit component.

unitSigmaBwd

Section titled “unitSigmaBwd”

Backward map of Unit × A ≅ A: pairs unit with a value.

unitSigmaRoundtrip1

Section titled “unitSigmaRoundtrip1”

Pairing with unit on the left and dropping it again gives back the value.

unitSigmaRoundtrip2

Section titled “unitSigmaRoundtrip2”

Dropping the unit component and pairing it back gives the original pair.

sigmaContrBaseFwd

Section titled “sigmaContrBaseFwd”

Over a contractible base, transports the fiber element of a dependent pair to the fiber at the center.

sigmaContrBaseBwd

Section titled “sigmaContrBaseBwd”

Over a contractible base, puts an element of the fiber at the center into the dependent sum.

sigmaContrBaseBwdFst

Section titled “sigmaContrBaseBwdFst”

The base point of an element built by sigmaContrBaseBwd is the center.

sigmaContrFiberFwd

Section titled “sigmaContrFiberFwd”

With contractible fibers, projects a dependent pair to its base point.

sigmaContrFiberBwd

Section titled “sigmaContrFiberBwd”

With contractible fibers, lifts a base point into the dependent sum using the center of its fiber.

sigmaContrFiberRoundtrip1

Section titled “sigmaContrFiberRoundtrip1”

Lifting a base point with sigmaContrFiberBwd and projecting it back gives the same point.

singlContrR

Section titled “singlContrR”

The singleton type of points y with a path x = y is contractible.

singlContrL

Section titled “singlContrL”

The singleton type of points y with a path y = x is contractible.

singlCenter

Section titled “singlCenter”

The center of the singleton type at x: x with the constant path.

singlCenterFst

Section titled “singlCenterFst”

The point of the singleton center at x is x.

sigmaBimap

Section titled “sigmaBimap”

Applies one function to each component of a pair.

sigmaBimapComp

Section titled “sigmaBimapComp”

Applying sigmaBimap twice equals applying it once with the composed functions.

sigmaBimapCompute

Section titled “sigmaBimapCompute”

sigmaBimap f g (a, b) computes to (f a, g b).

sigmaFirst

Section titled “sigmaFirst”

Applies a function to the first component of a pair, keeping the second.

sigmaSecond

Section titled “sigmaSecond”

Applies a function to the second component of a pair, keeping the first.

sigmaFirstCompute

Section titled “sigmaFirstCompute”

sigmaFirst f (a, b) computes to (f a, b).

sigmaSecondCompute

Section titled “sigmaSecondCompute”

sigmaSecond g (a, b) computes to (a, g b).

sigmaDiag

Section titled “sigmaDiag”

Diagonal map: duplicates a value into a pair.

sigmaDiagFst

Section titled “sigmaDiagFst”

The first component of the diagonal pair is the original value.

sigmaDiagSnd

Section titled “sigmaDiagSnd”

The second component of the diagonal pair is the original value.

sigmaDiagCompute

Section titled “sigmaDiagCompute”

sigmaDiag a computes to (a, a).

sigmaFanout

Section titled “sigmaFanout”

Applies two functions to the same input and pairs the results.

sigmaFanoutFst

Section titled “sigmaFanoutFst”

The first component of sigmaFanout f g a is f a.

sigmaFanoutSnd

Section titled “sigmaFanoutSnd”

The second component of sigmaFanout f g a is g a.

sigmaFanoutCompute

Section titled “sigmaFanoutCompute”

sigmaFanout f g a computes to (f a, g a).

sigmaCurry

Section titled “sigmaCurry”

Curries a function on dependent pairs into a two-argument dependent function.

sigmaUncurry

Section titled “sigmaUncurry”

Uncurries a two-argument dependent function into a function on dependent pairs.

sigmaCurryUncurry

Section titled “sigmaCurryUncurry”

Currying an uncurried function gives back the original function pointwise.

sigmaUncurryCurry

Section titled “sigmaUncurryCurry”

Uncurrying a curried function gives back the original function on every pair.

sigmaApply

Section titled “sigmaApply”

Applies a pair of functions componentwise to a pair of arguments.

sigmaApplyCompute

Section titled “sigmaApplyCompute”

sigmaApply (f, g) (a, b) computes to (f a, g b).

sigmaDup

Section titled “sigmaDup”

Applies one function to both components of a homogeneous pair.

sigmaDupCompute

Section titled “sigmaDupCompute”

sigmaDup f (a1, a2) computes to (f a1, f a2).

exists_intro

Section titled “exists_intro”

Introduces an existential from a witness and a proof that it satisfies the predicate.

exists_witness

Section titled “exists_witness”

The witness of an existential.

exists_proof

Section titled “exists_proof”

The proof that the witness of an existential satisfies the predicate.

exists_elim

Section titled “exists_elim”

Eliminates an existential by a function that works for every witness and proof.

exists_map

Section titled “exists_map”

Maps an existential along a fiberwise implication between predicates, keeping the witness.

existsMapCompute

Section titled “existsMapCompute”

exists_map f (a, pa) computes to (a, f a pa).

Section titled “sigmaBoolInjectFalse”

sigmaBoolInjectTrueFst

sigmaBoolInjectFalseFst

Section titled “sigmaBoolInjectFalseFst”

sigmaNatSuccFst

Section titled “sigmaNatSuccFst”

sigmaFlipFlip

Section titled “sigmaFlipFlip”

Flipping a function on pairs twice gives back the original function on every pair.

sigmaDistrib

Section titled “sigmaDistrib”

Distributes a product over a sum: A × (B + C) to (A × B) + (A × C).

sigmaUndistrib

Section titled “sigmaUndistrib”

Factors a sum of products: (A × B) + (A × C) to A × (B + C).

sigmaDistribRet

sigmaTriple

sigmaQuad

sigmaTripleFst

isSetSigmaNonDep2

sigmaConstProj2

sigmaIdFwd

sigmaIdBwd

sigmaOfPi_to_piOfSigma

congSigmaPath

etaMk

fstSigmaMapSnd

sndSigmaMapSnd

sigmaEmpty

sigmaEmptyFiber

boolNatPair
