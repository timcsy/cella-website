linsys

dot

Section titled “dot”

Dot product of two integer vectors, truncated to the shorter one.

vecAdd

Section titled “vecAdd”

Componentwise sum of two integer vectors, keeping the tail of the longer one.

vecScale

Section titled “vecScale”

Scales every component of an integer vector by a constant.

linMix4

Section titled “linMix4”

Ring identity used to split the dot product of a vector sum into two dot products.

linMix2

Section titled “linMix2”

Ring identity used to pull a scalar factor out of a dot product.

dotAdd

Section titled “dotAdd”

Dot product distributes over vector addition on the left.

dotScale

Section titled “dotScale”

Scaling the left vector scales the dot product by the same constant.

Solves

Section titled “Solves”

The proposition that x / f solves the system: every row satisfies dot a x = f * b.

combCoeffs

Section titled “combCoeffs”

Coefficient vector of the linear combination of the rows weighted by y.

combRhs

Section titled “combRhs”

Right-hand side of the linear combination of the rows weighted by y.

linMix3

Section titled “linMix3”

Ring identity used to factor the denominator out of a combined right-hand side.

combSound

Section titled “combSound”

Any solution of the system also satisfies every linear combination of its rows.

boolCode

Section titled “boolCode”

Maps true to Unit and false to Empty, used to separate the two booleans.

falseNeTrue

Section titled “falseNeTrue”

The boolean false is not equal to true.

isZeroVec

Section titled “isZeroVec”

Decides whether every component of an integer vector is zero.

isZeroIntSound

Section titled “isZeroIntSound”

Soundness of Int.isZero: if it returns true the integer is zero.

andTrueL

Section titled “andTrueL”

If a boolean conjunction is true, its left operand is true.

andTrueElimR

Section titled “andTrueElimR”

If a boolean conjunction is true, its right operand is true.

isZeroVecDot

Section titled “isZeroVecDot”

A vector recognized as zero by isZeroVec has zero dot product with any vector.

mulPosSuccZero

Section titled “mulPosSuccZero”

If a positive integer times c is zero, then c is zero.

linNoSolution

Section titled “linNoSolution”

Soundness of the no-solution certificate: a row combination with zero coefficients and nonzero right-hand side rules out every rational solution.

LinNoSolution

Section titled “LinNoSolution”

The statement that the system has no rational solution with a positive denominator.

linMix5

Section titled “linMix5”

Ring identity used to split a dot product over a sum on the right.

linMix6

Section titled “linMix6”

Ring identity used to pull a scalar factor out of the right vector of a dot product.

dotAddR

Section titled “dotAddR”

Dot product distributes over vector addition on the right.

dotScaleR

Section titled “dotScaleR”

Scaling the right vector scales the dot product by the same constant.

linShift

Section titled “linShift”

Moving a solution any multiple t along a direction in the kernel of the system yields another solution.

LinUnderdetermined

Section titled “LinUnderdetermined”

Underdetermined certificate: a rational solution together with a nonzero direction k satisfying Ak = 0.

linSubEq

Section titled “linSubEq”

Ring identity rewriting a as (a - c) + c.

subZeroEq

Section titled “subZeroEq”

If Int.isZero recognizes a - c as zero, then a equals c.

isScaledUnit

Section titled “isScaledUnit”

Decides whether a vector is c times the j-th unit vector (and has a j-th position).

scaledUnitDot

Section titled “scaledUnitDot”

A vector that is c times the j-th unit vector has dot product with z equal to c times the j-th component of z.

linUniqueCoord

Section titled “linUniqueCoord”

If a row combination turns the coefficients into (e+1) times the j-th unit vector, it pins down the j-th component of every solution.

linPos

Section titled “linPos”

Computes i + j by moving i onto j, the position handled by the i-th combination.

linPosZero

Section titled “linPosZero”

linPos i j equals the ordinary sum i + j.

allUnitsFrom

Section titled “allUnitsFrom”

Checks that each successive combination in the list yields a scaled unit vector at consecutive positions starting from j.

listNthL

Section titled “listNthL”

The i-th vector of a list of integer vectors, or the empty vector when out of range.

allUnitsNth

Section titled “allUnitsNth”

Extracts from allUnitsFrom that the i-th combination yields the scaled unit vector at position linPos i j.

linWidth

Section titled “linWidth”

Number of variables of the system, the length of its longest coefficient row.

LinUnique

Section titled “LinUnique”

Unique-solution certificate: a rational solution plus one row combination per variable that pins that variable down.

linUniqueAt

Section titled “linUniqueAt”

Reads a unique-solution certificate: the i-th combination determines the i-th component of every solution.

BoundOp

Section titled “BoundOp”

Comparison kinds for bounding a component of the unique solution: le, lt, ge, gt, eq, and absolute value at most.

boundCmp

Section titled “boundCmp”

Evaluates a bound comparison on two integers that already share a common denominator.

boundHolds

Section titled “boundHolds”

Checks whether x / (d+1) compares to num / (den+1) as the bound operator demands, by cross-multiplying.

LinBound

Section titled “LinBound”

Bound certificate: the system has a unique solution and its k-th component satisfies the given rational bound.
