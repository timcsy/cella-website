Position

Section titled “Position”

A place in the source: the module, and the line and the column, both counted from 1.

Position.unknown

Section titled “Position.unknown”

No known place.

Position.show

Section titled “Position.show”

The place p as module:line:column; empty when it is not known.

ErrorKind

Section titled “ErrorKind”

The kind of an error, defined by the package that makes the error: the package’s name and the kind’s name. Two kinds are the same when both names are.

ErrorKind.named

Section titled “ErrorKind.named”

The kind name defined by the package pkg.

ErrorKind.context

Section titled “ErrorKind.context”

The kind of a layer that says what was being done (r.context(msg)): not classified.

ErrorKind.other

Section titled “ErrorKind.other”

The kind of an error made of a value of another error type without a kind given (Error.of): not classified.

ErrorKind.same

Section titled “ErrorKind.same”

Whether the kinds a and b are the same.

Error

Section titled “Error”

The general error, in layers: errorLayer(message, kind, cause, at) is a layer with its message, its kind, the error it was caused by (none for the innermost layer) and the place where it was made.

Error.message

Section titled “Error.message”

The message of the outermost layer of e.

Error.kind

Section titled “Error.kind”

The kind of the outermost layer of e.

Error.cause

Section titled “Error.cause”

The error the outermost layer of e was caused by.

Error.at

Section titled “Error.at”

The place where the outermost layer of e was made.

Error.of

Section titled “Error.of”

An error made of x, a value of another error type: its message is toString(x), its kind kind, made at at (the place of the call when not given).

Error.wrap

Section titled “Error.wrap”

The error e with one more layer outside it saying what was being done: the message message, the kind ErrorKind.context, made at at (the place of the call when not given).

Error.is

Section titled “Error.is”

Whether a layer of e (the outermost or one it was caused by) has the kind k.

Error.root

Section titled “Error.root”

The innermost layer of e: the error the other layers were caused by.

Error.show

Section titled “Error.show”

The messages of the layers of e, from the outside in, separated by : .

Error.showWithPlace

Section titled “Error.showWithPlace”

The message m of a layer, then its place p in parentheses.

Error.showDetailed

Section titled “Error.showDetailed”

The layers of e, from the outside in, one a line, each with its place.

toStringError

Section titled “toStringError”

An error as the messages of its layers.

Result

Section titled “Result”

The result of a computation that can fail: ok(value), or err(error) with the error it failed with. Result(A) fails with the general Error.

Result.isOk

Section titled “Result.isOk”

Whether r holds a value.

Result.isErr

Section titled “Result.isErr”

Whether r failed.

Result.map

Section titled “Result.map”

f of the value of r; an error stays.

Result.mapErr

Section titled “Result.mapErr”

f of the error of r, to another error type; a value stays.

Result.context

Section titled “Result.context”

The error of r with one more layer, message, saying what was being done (Error.wrap), made at at (the place of the call when not given); a value stays.

Result.toOption

Section titled “Result.toOption”

The value of r, if it holds one.
