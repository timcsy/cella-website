IsAssoc

Section titled “IsAssoc”

The proposition that a binary operation is associative: x op (y op z) = (x op y) op z.

IsLeftUnit

Section titled “IsLeftUnit”

The proposition that e is a left unit for op: e op x = x.

IsRightUnit

Section titled “IsRightUnit”

The proposition that e is a right unit for op: x op e = x.

IsMonoid

Section titled “IsMonoid”

The proposition that (A, op, e) is a monoid: op is associative and e is a two-sided unit.

natAddIsAssoc

Section titled “natAddIsAssoc”

Natural-number addition is associative.

natAddIsLeftUnit

Section titled “natAddIsLeftUnit”

Zero is a left unit for natural-number addition.

natAddIsRightUnit

Section titled “natAddIsRightUnit”

Zero is a right unit for natural-number addition.

natAddIsMonoid

Section titled “natAddIsMonoid”

Natural numbers form a monoid under addition with unit zero.

natMulIsLeftUnit

Section titled “natMulIsLeftUnit”

One is a left unit for natural-number multiplication.

natMulIsRightUnit

Section titled “natMulIsRightUnit”

One is a right unit for natural-number multiplication.

IsLeftInverse

Section titled “IsLeftInverse”

The proposition that inv is a left inverse for op with unit e: inv x op x = e.

IsRightInverse

Section titled “IsRightInverse”

The proposition that inv is a right inverse for op with unit e: x op inv x = e.

IsGroup

Section titled “IsGroup”

The proposition that (A, op, e, inv) is a group: a monoid in which inv gives two-sided inverses.

addIntLeftInv

Section titled “addIntLeftInv”

Negation is a left inverse for integer addition: (-n) + n = 0.

addIntLeftInvPos

Section titled “addIntLeftInvPos”

Left inverse law for integer addition on non-negative integers: (-k) + k = 0.

addIntLeftInvPosStep

Section titled “addIntLeftInvPosStep”

Left inverse law for integer addition on positive integers: negsucc j + (j + 1) = 0.

addIntRightInv

Section titled “addIntRightInv”

Negation is a right inverse for integer addition: n + (-n) = 0.

addIntRightInvPos

Section titled “addIntRightInvPos”

Right inverse law for integer addition on non-negative integers: k + (-k) = 0.

addIntRightInvPosStep

Section titled “addIntRightInvPosStep”

Adding the natural number j + 1 to negsucc j gives zero.

addIntLeftInvNeg

Section titled “addIntLeftInvNeg”

Left inverse law for integer addition on negative integers: -(negsucc k) + negsucc k = 0.

addIntRightInvNeg

Section titled “addIntRightInvNeg”

Right inverse law for integer addition on negative integers: negsucc k + -(negsucc k) = 0.

addIntLeftId

Section titled “addIntLeftId”

Zero is a left identity for integer addition: 0 + n = n.

intAddIsLeftUnit

Section titled “intAddIsLeftUnit”

Zero is a left unit for integer addition.

intAddIsRightUnit

Section titled “intAddIsRightUnit”

Zero is a right unit for integer addition.

intAddIsLeftInverse

Section titled “intAddIsLeftInverse”

Negation gives left inverses for integer addition.

intAddIsRightInverse

Section titled “intAddIsRightInverse”

Negation gives right inverses for integer addition.

The proposition that a binary operation is commutative: x op y = y op x.

natAddIsComm

Section titled “natAddIsComm”

Natural-number addition is commutative.

natMulIsComm

Section titled “natMulIsComm”

Natural-number multiplication is commutative.

natMulIsAssoc

Section titled “natMulIsAssoc”

Natural-number multiplication is associative.

IsSemigroup

Section titled “IsSemigroup”

The proposition that (A, op) is a semigroup, i.e. op is associative.

natAddIsSemigroup

Section titled “natAddIsSemigroup”

Natural numbers form a semigroup under addition.

natMulIsSemigroup

Section titled “natMulIsSemigroup”

Natural numbers form a semigroup under multiplication.

natMulIsMonoid

Section titled “natMulIsMonoid”

Natural numbers form a monoid under multiplication with unit one.

IsDistribL

Section titled “IsDistribL”

The proposition that mul distributes over add from the left: x * (y + z) = x * y + x * z.

IsDistribR

Section titled “IsDistribR”

The proposition that mul distributes over add from the right: (x + y) * z = x * z + y * z.

IsRing

Section titled “IsRing”

The proposition that (A, add, mul, 0, 1, neg) is a ring: an additive group, a multiplicative monoid, and two-sided distributivity.

IsAbelianGroup

Section titled “IsAbelianGroup”

The proposition that (A, op, e, inv) is an abelian group: a group whose operation is commutative.

IsCommRing

Section titled “IsCommRing”

The proposition that (A, add, mul, 0, 1, neg) is a commutative ring: a ring with commutative multiplication.

natMulDistribR

Section titled “natMulDistribR”

Natural-number multiplication distributes over addition from the right: (x + y) * z = x * z + y * z.

natMulDistribL

Section titled “natMulDistribL”

Natural-number multiplication distributes over addition from the left: x * (y + z) = x * y + x * z.

PreservesOp

Section titled “PreservesOp”

PreservesUnit

Section titled “PreservesUnit”

The proposition that f sends the unit eA to the unit eB.

IsMonoidHom

idIsMonoidHom

compIsMonoidHom

Section titled “compIsMonoidHom”

The composite of two monoid homomorphisms is a monoid homomorphism.

nat_add_power

The n-th additive power of a natural number, i.e. n copies of x added together.

monoidPowerZero

Section titled “monoidPowerZero”

The zeroth power in a monoid is the unit: x^0 = e.

natMulIsRightDistrib

leAdd

IsZeroAnnihilatingL

natMulIsZeroAnnihilatingL

natIsCommSemiring
